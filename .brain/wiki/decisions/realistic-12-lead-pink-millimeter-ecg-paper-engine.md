# Realistic 12-Lead Pink Millimeter ECG Paper Engine

Context: User requested full 12-lead ECG display rendered on realistic pink millimeter grid paper with clinical printout parameters. Rationale: Standard clinical ECG printouts use a 4x3 matrix with calibration pulses and continuous Lead II rhythm strip on 1mm/5mm pink grid paper, along with administrative headers, filter parameters, and automated metric readouts (PR, QRS, QT/QTc, Axis). Impact: Authentic hospital ECG paper printout experience and export capabilities.
