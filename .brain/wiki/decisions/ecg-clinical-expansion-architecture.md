# ECG Clinical Expansion Architecture

Context: Need deep clinical integration for ECG module including 28 pathologies, gold standard AHA/ESC criteria, 7-step reading protocol, emergency drug dosing, red flag contraindications and Module 3 ER synchronization. Rationale: Medical students and doctors need clinical context and actionable emergency protocols beyond wave display. Impact: Full clinical fidelity from diagnostic waveform to emergency bedside orders.
