# ECG 9 Systematic Clinical Taxonomy

Context: Categorizing all ECG pathologies into 9 authoritative clinical cardiology systems based on Braunwald and VNHA. Rationale: Eliminates fragmented disease lists and enables systematic resident learning by anatomical region, electrophysiological mechanism, and urgency. Impact: Two-level hierarchical navigation across UI, database, and emergency protocols.
